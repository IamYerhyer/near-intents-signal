Panel 1 — The Decoupling Chart

Intents volume has grown into a $100M+/day flow; the NEAR price rose over the same year but with sharp swings volume does not explain — daily volume vs price correlation is r = 0.50 (n = 365 days), rising to r = 0.78 for 30-day rolling volume, so the two are related in trend but volume is not a mirror of price.

Sources: https://api.llama.fi/summary/dexs/near-intents?dataType=dailyVolume and the coins.llama.fi NEAR price chart (exact URL in data/key_stats.json). Code: rebuild.py → data/intents_daily_volume.csv, data/near_daily_price.csv.

Panel 2 — Flow vs Vault

A single month of Intents flow (trailing-30d volume) is many times larger than the entire value locked on the NEAR chain — NEAR increasingly looks like a settlement pipe, not a vault.

Sources: https://api.llama.fi/summary/dexs/near-intents?dataType=dailyVolume (rolling 30d sum computed in rebuild.py) and https://api.llama.fi/v2/historicalChainTvl/Near. Code: rebuild.py → data/intents_daily_volume.csv, data/near_chain_tvl.csv.

Panel 3 — Does Volume Lead Price?

Cross-correlation peaks at lag +2 days for 7-day rolling volume (r = +0.70) and lag +3 days for raw daily volume (r = +0.54), where a positive lag means volume was measured before price. Levels cross-correlation peaks at or near lag 0 — volume and price move together rather than volume going first; on this data volume is a coincident, not leading, indicator.

Robustness check (levels are trend-inflated, so this matters more): correlation between the 7-day % change in 7-day rolling volume and forward price returns — 1d: r = +0.11, 7d: r = -0.01, 14d: r = +0.03, 30d: r = +0.05. Weak or negative values here mean volume growth does not reliably precede price gains even if the levels curves peak at a positive lag.

Sources: https://api.llama.fi/summary/dexs/near-intents?dataType=dailyVolume and the coins.llama.fi NEAR price chart. Code: rebuild.py → data/leadlag.csv; full method, best lags and forward-return tests in data/key_stats.json under leadlag.

Panel 4 — Does the Token Capture It? (Value Capture)

Of every $1 of gross Intents fees, about $0.34 reached NEAR as captured revenue over the last 30 complete days (capture ratio 34.2%; all-time 16.1%) — an effective revenue take of 0.054% of volume and an annualised revenue run-rate of $25.6M: the flow is large, the slice is thin.

Mechanism: Since 2026-02-23, NEAR's captured Intents revenue is used to buy back $NEAR on the open market (not burned), per the DefiLlama NEAR Intents methodology: revenue is NEAR's net captured revenue, measured as NEAR/wNEAR swept into its revenue wallets, and since 2026-02-23 that captured revenue funds open-market buybacks. Source: https://defillama.com/protocol/near-intents

Sources: https://api.llama.fi/summary/fees/near-intents?dataType=dailyFees and https://api.llama.fi/summary/fees/near-intents?dataType=dailyRevenue; buyback mechanism per the DefiLlama NEAR Intents methodology page linked above. Code: rebuild.py → data/intents_daily_fees.csv (partial day flagged), data/intents_daily_revenue.csv; ratios in data/key_stats.json under value_capture.
